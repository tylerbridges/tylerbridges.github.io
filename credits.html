Forecasts & alerts

NOAA / National Weather Service — forecasts, hourly data, observed conditions from nearby stations, active alerts, and hazard guidance, all via the public api.weather.gov service.

Rain timing & air quality

Open-Meteo — next-two-hours rain and snow timing (15-minute NOAA HRRR model guidance) and the air-quality index (a model estimate, not EPA monitor readings). Data licensed under CC BY 4.0.

Radar imagery

NOAA nowCOAST — current and recent NEXRAD radar mosaic shown on the Radar page, when reachable.

Iowa Environmental Mesonet — the same radar mosaic, used automatically if nowCOAST can't be reached.

NOAA / NWS Weather Prediction Center map services — current national precipitation, weather-type, fronts, and excessive-rainfall forecast maps.

Map

OpenFreeMap & OpenMapTiles — vector map tiles. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors.

Rendered with MapLibre GL JS, an open-source mapping library.

Location search

Photon by komoot, built on OpenStreetMap data — turns a city, state, or ZIP into a map location.
