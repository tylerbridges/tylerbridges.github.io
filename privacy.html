Privacy Policy

No accounts, no server-side storage

This site has no backend, no user accounts, and no database. It runs entirely in your browser as static pages. Any preference it remembers — your saved location, appearance (light/dark/system), and time zone — is stored only in your own browser's localStorage and never transmitted to us or saved on any server we operate.

Location

If you allow it, your browser's own geolocation feature is used to find weather for your current position. That request goes directly from your browser to your device's location service — we never see or store it ourselves. You can also type in a city, state, or ZIP instead of sharing your location at any time.

Third-party services

To show forecasts, radar, and maps, your browser makes requests directly to a handful of external services — the National Weather Service, Open-Meteo, the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap, Esri, and Photon (a location-search service built on OpenStreetMap data). See Credits for the full list. Those requests (for example, the coordinates being looked up) are sent straight from your browser to that service, not routed through us, so each provider's own privacy practices apply to that request. We don't control what they do with it.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any information from them.
