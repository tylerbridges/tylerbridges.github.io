Privacy Policy

No accounts, no server-side storage

This site has no backend, no user accounts, and no database. It runs entirely in your browser as static pages, so nothing it remembers is ever transmitted to us or saved on any server we operate.

What it keeps in your own browser's localStorage:

  • the location you're currently viewing, including its coordinates and place name;
  • any locations you save, and which one you've starred as a favorite;
  • a cache of the National Weather Service grid lookup for those coordinates (kept for up to three days), so the site doesn't re-request it on every visit;
  • a compact copy of the last forecast you saw for up to five locations (daily highs, lows, and rain chances), so the forecast cards can tell you what changed since you last checked;
  • a few minutes' worth of recently downloaded forecast, observation, and alert data, kept only for the current browser tab (session storage) so moving between pages doesn't download it again;
  • your settings: appearance, time zone, temperature unit, wind-speed unit, time format, "feels like" display, auto-refresh interval, and startup location;
  • three radar preferences: animation speed, zoom level, and the selected forecast-map area.

Clearing this site's data in your browser removes all of it.

Location

The site only asks for your location when you press the location (arrow) button; until then it shows a default location. If you allow it, your browser's own geolocation feature is used to find weather for your current position. We have no server, so your location never reaches us — but two things do happen with it, and you should know both. It is saved in your browser (see above) so the site can reopen where you left off, and, only while you've granted location permission and are using your current location, it is re-checked in the background on later visits. And the coordinates are sent to the weather and map services listed below, because that's how the forecast for your spot is fetched.

You can type in a city, state, or ZIP instead of sharing your location at any time.

Third-party services

To show forecasts, radar, and maps, your browser makes requests directly to a handful of external services — the National Weather Service, NOAA nowCOAST, the NOAA/NWS Weather Prediction Center map services, the Iowa Environmental Mesonet, Open-Meteo (rain timing and air quality), OpenFreeMap/OpenStreetMap, Photon (a location-search service built on OpenStreetMap data), and jsDelivr (a public CDN that serves the map library used on the Radar page). See Credits for the full list. Those requests (for example, the coordinates being looked up) are sent straight from your browser to that service, not routed through us, so each provider's own privacy practices apply to that request. We don't control what they do with it.

Cookies & tracking

This site does not use cookies, analytics, or advertising trackers. If that ever changes, this policy will be updated first.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect any information from them.
